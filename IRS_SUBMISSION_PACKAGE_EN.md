Dear Tax Examiner (ID #0361000000):

I, Eliana Machado de Almeida, respectfully submit the following response to IRS Letter 566-T dated May 18, 2026. The examination concerns the Schedule A mortgage interest deduction of $114,034 claimed on my 2024 Form 1040. I have conducted a thorough review of all source documents and the reconciliation is fully resolved.

**I. Good Faith Statement**

I reported every item of income and every deduction on my 2024 return in good faith, based upon documents provided by my institutional lenders. The $114,034 on Schedule A, Line 8a corresponds precisely to actual amounts on Forms 1098 issued by AmWest Funding Corp ($95,134, Box 1) and Radius Financial Group ($18,900, Box 6). No figure was fabricated, estimated, or inflated.

I also disclose voluntarily that the Radius mortgage interest of $1,869.86 (Form 1098, Box 1) was inadvertently omitted from Schedule A — an outcome that understates my deduction by $1,870 and is inconsistent with any intent to overclaim.

The overstatement identified herein arises from two technical misunderstandings of complex post-TCJA provisions: (1) I did not apply the IRC §163(h)(3)(B) $750,000 qualified loan limit to post-2017 home acquisition debt, and (2) the Radius refinance points were treated as fully deductible in 2024 rather than amortized ratably under IRC §461(g)(2). These are misunderstandings of law and procedure, not misrepresentations of fact.

**II. Reconciliation — What Was Claimed**

The $114,034 on Schedule A consists of: AmWest interest $95,134 (Form 1098, Box 1) + Radius points $18,900 (Form 1098, Box 6) = $114,034. The $1,869.86 Radius interest was omitted from Schedule A as filed.

**III. IRC §163(h)(3)(B) — $750,000 Loan Limit**

Both mortgages were originated after December 15, 2017 and are subject to the post-TCJA $750,000 qualified loan limit. Using the Publication 936 average-balance method:

- AmWest average balance: $519,744 | Radius average balance: $420,000
- Combined average balance (Form 14900, Line 7): **$939,744**
- Qualified loan limit (Line 11): **$750,000**
- Deductible ratio (Line 14): $750,000 ÷ $939,744 = **79.8%**
- Deductible interest (Line 15): $97,004 × 79.8% = **$77,409**

**IV. Radius Points — Amortization Required**

The Radius loan retired the AmWest purchase mortgage six days after origination — this is a refinancing. Under IRC §461(g)(2), the $18,900 in points must be amortized over the 30-year loan term. The correct 2024 deductible amount, after the loan-limit ratio, is approximately **$67**. The original return treated the full $18,900 as currently deductible; this is conceded to be incorrect.

**V. Voluntary Concession**

| Item | As Filed | Corrected | Authority |
|------|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Deductible mortgage interest (Form 14900, Line 15) | $114,034 | $77,409 | IRC §163(h)(3)(B) |
| Deductible Radius points — 2024 (amortized, ratio-adjusted) | $18,900 | $67 | IRC §461(g)(2) |
| **Total corrected Schedule A mortgage deduction** | **$114,034** | **$77,476** | |
| **Net reduction in Schedule A** | | **($36,558)** | |

This reduction in itemized deductions increases my Qualified Business Income Deduction under IRC §199A from $5,196 to approximately $12,508. Net additional taxable income is approximately $29,246, and estimated additional tax is approximately $3,510. Exact figures will be reflected on Form 1040-X, which I am prepared to file.

I respectfully request that the Examiner advise whether the correction will be processed through this correspondence examination or whether a separate Form 1040-X filed under separate cover is required.
