### Applicable Law

IRC §163(h)(3)(B)(ii): Home acquisition debt incurred after December 15, 2017 is subject to a $750,000 qualified loan limit ($375,000 for married filing separately).

Both mortgages in this case were incurred after December 15, 2017:
- AmWest Funding Corp (originated 10/20/2023) — post-TCJA acquisition debt
- Radius Financial Group (originated 11/13/2024) — post-TCJA acquisition debt (refinance of AmWest)

The Radius loan refinanced the AmWest loan. Per IRS Publication 936, the amount treated as home acquisition debt for the refinanced mortgage cannot exceed the outstanding principal balance of the old mortgage immediately before the refinancing. AmWest balance on 11/13/2024 ≈ $1,033,587. Since $840,000 < $1,033,587, the full Radius loan qualifies as home acquisition debt. ✓

---

### Average Balance Calculations

**IRS Publication 936 Method:** Average balance = (Balance at 1/1 + Balance at 12/31) ÷ 2. For loans not outstanding the full year, the balance is $0 for the period before origination or after payoff.

**AmWest Funding Corp:**
- Balance on 1/1/2024: $1,039,488 (per Form 1098, Box 2)
- Balance on 12/31/2024: $0 (paid in full 11/19/2024; per Loan Activity Report)
- Average balance: ($1,039,488 + $0) ÷ 2 = **$519,744**

**Radius Financial Group:**
- Balance on 1/1/2024: $0 (originated 11/13/2024; did not exist at year start)
- Balance on 12/31/2024: $840,000 (per Form 1098, Box 2; loan origination balance)
- Average balance: ($0 + $840,000) ÷ 2 = **$420,000**

# SECTION 4 — POINTS DEDUCTIBILITY ANALYSIS

## Legal Framework

**IRS Publication 936 / IRC §461(g):**
- Points paid on a loan to **purchase** a principal residence are generally fully deductible in the year paid (meeting specific conditions).
- Points paid on a **refinance** must be deducted ratably over the life of the loan.

## Classification of Radius Loan

The Radius Financial Group loan (originated 11/13/2024, $840,000) was used to pay off the AmWest Funding Corp purchase-money mortgage (paid off 11/19/2024). This is a **refinancing transaction**, not a home purchase.

Supporting evidence:
- The original home was purchased 10/20/2023 with AmWest financing
- The AmWest loan was retired 6 days after the Radius loan originated
- The Radius loan balance ($840,000) is less than the AmWest payoff balance ($1,033,587)
- The property address is the same for both loans

**Conclusion:** Radius points are NOT fully deductible in 2024. They must be amortized over the term of the Radius loan. The loan term must be verified from the Mortgage Note.

## Points Amortization Calculation

The exact Radius points amount remains unresolved ($1,890 or $18,900 per conflicting documents). The Mortgage Note and actual Form 1098 must be obtained. For reference, both scenarios are shown below assuming a 30-year (360-month) loan term:

**Scenario A — Points = $1,890:**
- Monthly amortization: $1,890 ÷ 360 = $5.25/month
- Months in 2024 (11/13/2024–12/31/2024 ≈ 1.6 months): ~$8.40
- Loan limit ratio applied (×0.798): ~**$7** deductible in 2024

**Scenario B — Points = $18,900:**
- Monthly amortization: $18,900 ÷ 360 = $52.50/month
- Months in 2024 (~1.6 months): ~$84.00
- Loan limit ratio applied (×0.798): ~**$67** deductible in 2024
