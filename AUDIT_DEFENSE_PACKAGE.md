### Legal Basis

IRC §163(h)(3)(B)(ii): For home acquisition debt incurred **after December 15, 2017**, the qualified loan limit is **$750,000** ($375,000 married filing separately). Interest attributable to debt above this limit is treated as personal interest and is not deductible under IRC §163(h)(1).

**Both mortgages were incurred after December 15, 2017:**
- AmWest Funding Corp — originated 10/20/2023
- Radius Financial Group — originated 11/13/2024 (refinance of AmWest)

**Refinance qualification:** Per IRS Publication 936, the home acquisition debt on a refinanced mortgage is limited to the outstanding principal balance of the original mortgage immediately before the refinancing. AmWest balance on 11/12/2024 (day before Radius closing): $1,033,586.96. Radius loan balance: $840,000. Since $840,000 < $1,033,586.96, the full Radius loan qualifies as home acquisition debt. ✓

# SECTION 4 — POINTS DEDUCTIBILITY ANALYSIS

## 4A. Legal Framework — Refinance Points

**IRC §461(g)(2) and IRS Publication 936:**
Points paid in connection with a **home purchase** mortgage may generally be fully deducted in the year paid (if certain conditions are met — cash paid at closing, no borrowing of points, used to buy or improve main home, etc.).

Points paid in connection with a **refinancing** must be deducted ratably over the life of the loan. They cannot be fully deducted in the year paid.

## 4B. Classification of Radius Financial Group Loan

The Radius loan (originated 11/13/2024, $840,000) was used to pay off the AmWest purchase mortgage (paid off 11/19/2024). This is a **refinancing** of the original home purchase mortgage, not a new home purchase.

**Evidence of refinance:**
- Home was originally purchased 10/20/2023 with AmWest
- AmWest loan retired 6 days after Radius originated
- Radius balance ($840,000) less than AmWest payoff balance ($1,033,587)
- Same property secures both loans
- No new property acquisition occurred in 2024

**Classification: REFINANCE ✓ — Points must be amortized under IRC §461(g)(2)**

## 4C. Points Amortization Calculation — 2024

**Confirmed points:** $18,900 (Radius Form 1098, Box 6)
**Loan term:** Must be confirmed from Radius Mortgage Note (assumed 30 years / 360 months for this calculation)
**Origination date:** 11/13/2024
**Months in 2024:** November 13 – December 31, 2024 ≈ 1.57 months (47 days ÷ 30)

---

**(1) Taxpayer Good Faith — No Fabrication or Inflation of Any Figure**

I reported mortgage interest based entirely upon Forms 1098 issued by two regulated institutional lenders: AmWest Funding Corp and Radius Financial Group. Every dollar claimed on Schedule A traces directly to a lender-issued document. At no point did I estimate, fabricate, or inflate any figure. The errors identified herein involve complex provisions of the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act of 2017.

I further note that a secondary error caused the Radius interest of $1,869.86 to be **omitted** from Schedule A — reducing my claimed deduction below its correct amount by $1,870. This outcome is inconsistent with any intent to over-claim and is being disclosed voluntarily.

---

**(2) Reconciliation of $114,034 — Fully Resolved**

The Schedule A deduction of $114,034 consists of:
- AmWest Funding Corp — mortgage interest: **$95,134** (Form 1098, verified via Loan Activity Report)
- Radius Financial Group — points paid at closing: **$18,900** (Form 1098, Box 6, confirmed)
- Radius Financial Group — mortgage interest: **$0 claimed / $1,870 actual** (inadvertently omitted; disclosed herein)

---

**(3) AmWest Interest — $95,134 Verified**

The AmWest interest has been confirmed payment-by-payment against the Loan Activity Report (Loan #2000086400). Eleven monthly payments plus the final payoff interest on November 19, 2024, total $95,134.47, in precise agreement with Form 1098, Box 1. No additional deductible interest is present in the payoff transaction.

---

**(4) Radius Loan — Refinance; Points Must Be Amortized**

The Radius loan retired the AmWest purchase mortgage six days after origination. This is a refinancing. Under IRC §461(g)(2) and IRS Publication 936, the $18,900 in points must be deducted ratably over the 30-year loan term. The correct 2024 deductible amount, after applying the loan-limit ratio, is approximately **$67**. The original return treated the full $18,900 as currently deductible; this treatment is conceded to be incorrect.

---

**(5) IRC §163(h)(3)(B) — $750,000 Loan Limit Not Applied**

Both loans were originated after December 15, 2017. The post-TCJA $750,000 qualified loan limit applies. Form 14900 was not completed on the original return. The completed Form 14900 (Exhibit 11) establishes:
- Combined average mortgage balance (Line 12): $939,744
- Qualified loan limit (Line 11): $750,000
- Deductible ratio (Line 14): 0.798
- **Correct deductible interest (Line 15): $77,409**
- Non-deductible personal interest (Line 16): $19,595

---

**(6) Voluntary Concession**

I respectfully concede that the correct Schedule A mortgage deduction for 2024 is **$77,476** ($77,409 deductible interest per Form 14900, plus $67 of amortized and ratio-adjusted Radius points), compared to $114,034 as filed. The net overstatement is **$36,558**.

As a result of this reduction, my Qualified Business Income Deduction under IRC §199A increases from $5,196 to approximately $12,508. The net additional taxable income, after the QBID offset, is approximately $29,246. I am prepared to file Form 1040-X to reflect all corrections.
