**CORRECTED DEDUCTION CALCULATION**

Both loans are post-TCJA (originated after December 15, 2017) and are subject to the $750,000 qualified home acquisition debt limit under IRC §163(h)(3)(B)(ii).

Using the average balance methodology prescribed by IRS Publication 936:

- AmWest average balance for 2024: ($1,039,488 + $0) ÷ 2 = **$519,744**
- Radius average balance for 2024: ($0 + $840,000) ÷ 2 = **$420,000**
- **Combined average balance (Form 14900, Line 7): $939,744**
- **Limitation ratio: $750,000 ÷ $939,744 = 79.8%**

**Mortgage Interest — IRC §163(h)(3):**

$95,134 + $1,870 = **$97,004**
Deductible interest: $97,004 × 79.8% = **$77,409**

**Radius Refinance Points — IRC §461(g):**
